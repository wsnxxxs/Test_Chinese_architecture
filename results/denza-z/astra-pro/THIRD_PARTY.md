# Third-party notices

## Three.js r140

`public/vendor/three.r140.min.js` is the unmodified Three.js r140 UMD runtime,
identified by its retained source license header and `THREE.REVISION === '140'`.
The MIT license is included in `public/vendor/LICENSE.three.txt`.

`public/vendor/three.module.js` is a project-generated ES module bridge to the
same local runtime. No network/CDN library is required.

The renderer was obtained from a locally available copy of the Three.js runtime
included in the trimesh viewer template. No trimesh viewer code, car mesh,
example scene, HDR image, image texture, or third-party font is used by this app.
